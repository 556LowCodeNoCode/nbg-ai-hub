**Deployment** is the step that makes a change real for other people: moving it from your laptop to somewhere colleagues or customers can reach.

Worth separating three things that feel similar and aren't:

- **Saving** — the change exists on your machine.
- **Committing** — the change is recorded in the project's history.
- **Deploying** — the change is now live for other people.

You can do the first two all day without anyone else being affected. Only the third changes what others see, which is why it usually involves a review and an approval step.

For most work in this hub you'll never deploy anything — a summary, a chart or a spreadsheet is finished when the file is. The word shows up because Claude Code can also do the deploying, and because "it works on my machine" and "it's live" are very different claims.
